### Topic 29 | Interviews vs Contextual Inquiry

* Interviews = What users say
* Contextual Inquiry = What actually users do

"Both of the above are user research, but they answer different questions."

** USE INTERVIEW WHEN: **
  - Motivation & intent
  - Past Experience
  - Opinion, beliefs, frustrations
  - Observing user problem

** USE Contextual Inquiries WHEN: **
  - Observing user action
  - Watching real behavior
  - Seeing workarounds and friction

### Topic 33 | What is Quantitative Research?

The basic of Quantitative Research:
  - How often something happens?
  - How many users are effected?
  - Where patterns exists?
Because everything is converted into numbers, quantitative research is easier to evaluate and analyse. like Satisfaction or Rating converted or calculated into numbers.

** Benefits of Quantitative Research**
  - **Measure Scale:** How likly are you refer this store to yours friends? (1-lower, 5-highest)
  - **Compare Versions:** Is Design A better than Design B? 
  - **Track change Over Time:** How satisfied do you feel about this product? (1-lower, 5-highest)
  - **Priortize Problems:** Out of these 3 problems, which one would you set highest priority?
